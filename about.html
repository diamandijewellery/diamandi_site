The Artist

About

Diamandi is a London-based grillz artist specialising in hand-carved wax to metal pieces — grillz, rings, and bespoke jewellery.

Based in Hatton Garden and with Greek heritage rooted in a silversmithing village, jewellery is part of the DNA of the brand.

The focus is always on detail — producing pieces that are refined, beautiful, and built to be worn. For grillz, that means safe materials and a fit that works.

Diamandi is committed to the best work possible and does not use any toxic materials or processes.

Always looking to push further, across grillz, rings, and beyond.
